# EXP-003 natural-vocal successor plan

Status: planned experiment only.

Goal: preserve the compact, compositional lessons from EXP-002 while designing a smoother and more naturally vocal acoustic language.

Principles:
- Chordic-Language stays authoritative.
- Keep EXP-002 unchanged as history.
- Routine conversation target remains about 4-10 seconds.
- Reduce literal-English fallback.
- Prefer reusable semantic tokens and compact grammar.
- Test relative pitch, contours, intervals, and speaker-normalized registers.
- Explore connected hums, glides, low resonant body, purr-like vibration, whale-like continuity, and natural bird-like contour instead of isolated beeps.
- Keep important semantic contrasts easy to distinguish.
- Keep future streaming phone/laptop recognition as a design constraint.
- Test multiple human vocal ranges.
- Separate timing, symbolic collisions, acoustic distinguishability, and subjective listening evidence.

Rocky presentation target:
- Chordic starts first.
- English follows about 0.5-1.0 seconds later.
- Chordic is slightly quieter.
- Chordic should normally finish shortly before English.
- Do not make English unnaturally slow merely to force timing alignment.

Benchmark real conversation samples such as greetings/name exchange, simple math, favorite-color discussion, food-preference questions, reassurance, technical explanations, and the existing historical seed phrases.

EXP-003 should create new candidate data, benchmark data, generated audio, reproducible metrics, failure notes, and tests. Do not promote canonical grammar, dictionary, or phonology without evidence.
